## iSchool, University of Illinois (2024 - 2026)

Dulf entered the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in August 2022 through the Division of General (Exploratory) Studies — undeclared, on purpose. That home for not-knowing-yet lasted through January 2024, when he was admitted to the iSchool while finishing his Spanish minor in Granada.

youtube:7mgG9dN-IG4

The University of Illinois School of Information Sciences (iSchool), established in 1893, stands as a global leader in shaping the future of information, research, and public engagement. Within the broader excellence of the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, the iSchool is dedicated to transformative learning that produces alumni poised to lead the digital age. Boasting a world-class faculty, the school integrates a focus on data science and human-centered design that fuels innovation—a synergy perfectly embodied by Dulf’s pursuit of a dual degree in Information Sciences and Data Science. Beyond its specialized curriculum, students benefit from a vibrant campus life, the vast academic network of a premier research institution, and impactful global experiences. For Dulf, this extended to a Spanish minor and an immersive study abroad semester in Granada, Spain, where the iSchool acceptance reached him in January 2024. The school defines pre-eminence through its commitment to diversity, equity, and the land-grant mission, equipping graduates to ethically manage and interpret the world's information.

Rather than blindly optimizing algorithms for corporate efficiency, Dulf leveraged the iSchool's interdisciplinary curriculum to champion transparent, accountable technologies, such as engineering the "glass-box" AI system HarmonyForge to preserve user sovereignty. Guided by faculty like Dr. Madelyn Sanfilippo and Dr. Yun Huang, he anchored his technical data science capabilities within rigorous ethical frameworks, ultimately preparing him to act as a "Data Gardener" who scales systems in tandem with human dignity rather than unchecked capability.

## Maine West High School (2018 - 2022)


![](/images/about/education/ce1dd9354c0954fc9e8332c171a2d532.jpg)

Maine West High School, also known as Maine West or MWHS, is a public four-year high school situated in Des Plaines, Illinois, a northwest suburb of Chicago. Established in 1959, it forms part of Maine Township High School District 207, along with Maine East High School and Maine South High School. Serving a substantial portion of Des Plaines and Rosemont, Maine West is renowned for its academic excellence, offering a range of Advanced Placement courses and foreign language options. The school's history reflects thoughtful planning, with its design featuring a central core and separate classroom wings. Beyond academics, Maine West boasts a rich athletic tradition, competing in various sports and achieving notable successes, including state championships in badminton, baseball, and girls' basketball. The school's commitment to excellence is further exemplified by awards recognizing outstanding performance and transformative educational experiences for its students. With a diverse range of notable alumni, Maine West High School continues to contribute to its community and uphold a legacy of academic, artistic, and athletic achievements.

## Algonquin Middle School (2015 - 2018)

![](/images/about/education/61e253ac4a3c55f4c935bc133c70c594.jpg)

Algonquin Middle School, nestled within the vibrant community it serves, is committed to providing a holistic and enriching educational experience for its students. The school's multifaceted approach encompasses academic excellence, community engagement, and student well-being. Underpinning its mission is a robust learning framework based on a consistent, standards-based curriculum and materials, delivered by skilled educators. Algonquin Middle School employs a Multi-tiered System of Support (MTSS), tailoring interventions to address academic and social-emotional needs. The school's commitment to inclusive learning is evident in its comprehensive offerings, including after-school activities, athletics, clubs, and community partnerships. Algonquin Middle School stands as a beacon of academic achievement, fostering a culture of collaboration, engagement, and continuous improvement to prepare students for success in a rapidly evolving world.

## Forest Elementary School (2013 - 2015)


![](/images/about/education/6c3a22f1e971b6fae3eecb16fde83330.jpg)

Forest Elementary School, a cornerstone of educational excellence, is committed to fostering a nurturing environment for students to thrive academically and emotionally. From its comprehensive curriculum aligned with Illinois State Standards, including the Common Core for English Language Arts and Mathematics, to its innovative learning approaches such as Kagan cooperative structures, Forest Elementary ensures a high-quality educational experience. The school employs a multi-tiered system of support (MTSS) that recognizes the interconnectedness of academic and behavioral well-being. Forest Elementary caters to individual student needs through tiers of intervention, including Tier 1 core learning, Tier 2 additional support, and Tier 3 intensive intervention. Engaging families in the learning journey, the school emphasizes open communication and involvement, offering resources such as annual reports, digital learning tools, and support programs. Forest Elementary School is not just an educational institution; it's a community dedicated to empowering students for a successful future.

## Good Shepherd Christian Academy (2009 - 2013)


![](/images/about/education/2ee8df5b747a55a9c0709dabf79a17cf.jpg)

Good Shepherd Christian Academy, situated in Butuan City, Philippines, is dedicated to providing a quality education grounded in the School of Tomorrow System. Utilizing an individualized, Bible-based approach, the academy places a premium on holistic development. The school fosters a supportive environment where students engage with academic excellence, character development, and spiritual growth. Regular updates on school activities, student achievements, and character traits highlight the vibrant and inclusive community. With a focus on gratitude, resilience, and faith, Good Shepherd Christian Academy strives to instill values that extend beyond the classroom, preparing students to face life's challenges with courage and a strong moral foundation.
