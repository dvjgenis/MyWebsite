## Responsible AI Presentation

![](/images/initiatives/speaking/ffa5cbd533830387fb78a1fba257c05b.png)

![](/images/initiatives/speaking/4b4c5013f256b414735143e56a4d89a2.png)

I designed and led a comprehensive, hour-long workshop titled "Smarter, Safer, and Greener AI" for the entire staff at the Center of Concern . As the sole presenter, I spearheaded this organization-wide initiative to educate team members on core AI mechanics, strict data privacy governance, and the environmental footprint associated with large language models. The Center of Concern is a Des Plaines-based non-profit dedicated to supporting older adults and families through housing assistance, community resources, and senior care programs. Their work focuses on empowering vulnerable community members to maintain independence, housing stability, and an improved quality of life.

To bridge theory with action, I also directed an interactive hands-on lab training the staff on privacy-conscious, grounded tools—including Duck.ai , Asta.Allen.Ai , and Gemini Notebook —to directly optimize non-profit workflows. Leading this end-to-end training was a rewarding opportunity to drive responsible digital transformation, elevate technical capacity, and foster data stewardship across the agency.

## HarmonyForge: Architecting Human-Centered Explainable AI

![](/images/initiatives/speaking/df5fc0c2eaa602073fe125e5e78cf005.jpg)

![](/images/initiatives/speaking/43a573f667159f34e071ba64d5525a84.jpg)

In April 2026, Shivam Patel and I presented our capstone research, HarmonyForge, at both the iSchool Student Showcase and the Undergraduate Research Symposium . Serving as the Lead Researcher and Full-Stack AI Engineer alongside Shivam's frontend expertise, we demonstrated our solution to the "Repair Phase Bottleneck" caused by probabilistic AI music models. Our presentation at the iSchool Showcase focused on reducing the mechanical toil of music arrangement by applying an axiomatic, rule-based generative engine. Later in the month, at the Undergraduate Research Symposium, we expanded on our methodology under the mentorship of Dr. Yun Huang and Dr. Jeff Ginger, illustrating how our platform bridges this gap using a deterministic SATB constraint solver and a LangChain-powered Theory Inspector. By walking attendees through our RiffScore-based tactile sandbox, we showed how HarmonyForge preserves expressive sovereignty for musicians. We proved that integrating glass-box explainability and strict music theory taxonomies fosters deductive trust and prevents generative hallucinations. Sharing this sociotechnical architecture with the UIUC community was a deeply rewarding milestone in my dual degree journey in Information Sciences and Data Science, highlighting the tangible impact of human-centered, explainable AI.

## Graduation Speech

youtube:MzK0BzSkbj0

![](/images/initiatives/speaking/37b085c2cf880e7bb979e7d7469e1ad9.jpg)

![](/images/initiatives/speaking/b89d515b8d260d795f3ddb454e49a246.jpg)

I had the honor of delivering the closing address at the Maine West High School Graduation Ceremony for the Class of 2022. I crafted my speech into two distinctive parts. The first segment centered on congratulating my fellow graduates and reflecting on our collective journey through high school. Notably, I highlighted our resilience during the challenges of our junior year amidst the COVID-19 lockdown and our efforts to return to stability in our senior year.

The second part, often regarded as the highlight, shifted the focus to the commendable diversity present within the school, spanning ethnicity, sexual identities, race, political perspectives, schools of thought, and social classes. In addressing this, I shared insights into my own identity as an immigrant, expressing profound appreciation for the diverse environment. Emphasizing the significance and uniqueness of this setting, especially when contrasted with my background in a larger monoculture, corroborated the pivotal role of diversity in shaping my educational experience in the United States.
