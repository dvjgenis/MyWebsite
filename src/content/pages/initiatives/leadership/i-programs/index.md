## Innovation - 12.03.2022

![](/images/initiatives/leadership/i-programs/8695d4f75a5403fd4639a1e7293b7f19.jpg)

The Rolls-Royce Innovation program hones skills in innovation, creativity, and diverse problem-solving techniques. We gained insights into our dominant problem-solving style's influence on organizations and people. This i-Program, generously named after Rolls-Royce Corporation, is a catalyst for honing innovation and change management competencies, equipping us for future challenges.

## Imprint - 02.25.2023

![](/images/initiatives/leadership/i-programs/af2ed5ab575874e3156a5e0a1a1a39df.jpg)
![](/images/initiatives/leadership/i-programs/a94fd77dae4e8a1dada99b9196d3faed.jpg)

The Imprint program was a transformative journey that sharpened my skills in self-management during times of change. It underscored the significance of personal and professional networks, emphasizing their role in achieving success across various environments. The wisdom shared by accomplished alumni left a mark on my leadership development, as well as networking goals.
