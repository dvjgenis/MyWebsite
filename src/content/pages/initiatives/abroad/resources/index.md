Let this guide be your foundational or go-to companion for maximizing your study abroad experience! 🌍✈️

Chase Sapphire Preferred® Card

The Chase Sapphire Preferred® Card is a top-rated travel rewards card with an annual fee of $95. Earn 60,000 bonus points after meeting the initial spending requirement, which can be redeemed for travel, cashback, or gift cards. It's ideal for frequent travelers with excellent or good credit who want to maximize value through flexible rewards.

Bank of America® Travel Rewards Credit Card

The Bank of America® Travel Rewards Credit Card is a no-annual-fee option for travelers with good or excellent credit. After meeting the spending requirement, you earn 25,000 points, which you can redeem for travel expenses like flights and hotels. It’s a great card for those seeking simplicity and no foreign transaction fees.

Capital One Venture Rewards Credit Card

The Capital One Venture Rewards Credit Card offers impressive travel perks for an annual fee of $95. After qualifying purchases, you'll earn 75,000 bonus miles, and you'll enjoy flexible redemption options, including travel credits and transfers to airline partners. It's perfect for frequent travelers with excellent or good credit.

Capital One VentureOne Rewards Credit Card

The Capital One VentureOne Rewards Credit Card provides travel rewards with no annual fee. After meeting the initial spend requirement, you can earn 20,000 bonus miles, making it a great choice for occasional travelers seeking straightforward rewards and no foreign transaction fees.

Aliatar

Housed in a historic cinema building on Calle Recogidas, this venue transforms into a vibrant nightclub and event space hosting parties and concerts. I went Salsa dancing a lot here!

Daly's Pub

Formerly known as Paddy's, this authentic Irish pub on Calle Santa Escolástica is a cozy staple for live Celtic music and Guinness. Say hi to Steve for me!

Hannigan & Sons

Located on Calle Cetti Meriem, this spacious Irish pub is a major hub for international students to watch sports matches while enjoying a wide beer selection. My favorite karaoke place!

Mae West Granada

Situated in the Neptuno Shopping Center, this theatrical, multi-room nightclub features distinct themed areas like a Havana tavern and offers diverse music styles from reggaeton to house.

Capítulo 29 (C29)

A Christian community center and church that serves as a popular social hub for international students, known for hosting free weekly dinners and language exchange events.

Heladería Los Italianos

A legendary seasonal ice cream parlor on Gran Vía, famous for its traditional gelato and signature "Cassata" ice cream cake.

I Need Coffee

A specialty Italian coffee shop in the Realejo district that is a favorite study spot, offering high-quality 100% Arabica coffee, vegan options, and gluten-free treats.

Calle Gonzalo Gallas

This street near the Science Faculty is the epicenter of the "student tapas" scene, lined with bars serving massive, meal-sized free tapas with every drink.

Best Life Experience

A travel agency specifically for international students, organizing energetic, affordable group trips to destinations like Morocco, Portugal, and the Alpujarras.

Emycet

Another major student-focused travel and event organization offering cultural excursions, ski trips to Sierra Nevada, and social mixers.

Centro de Artes Escénicas

Located in the Zaidín neighborhood, this professional performing arts school offers training in various dance disciplines, including flamenco and urban dance. I learned how to dance here!

Mirador de San Nicolás

The most iconic viewpoint in the Albaicín, offering the classic postcard view of the Alhambra with the Sierra Nevada mountains in the background, often accompanied by street musicians.
